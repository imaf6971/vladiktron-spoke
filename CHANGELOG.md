# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac and your own copy of Elektron's OS 1.13.

- Runs the Model:Cycles OS 1.13 on a virtual board, with its screen, keys, knobs, pads and LEDs
- Audio out to the output chosen in Settings
- MIDI over USB and the TRS jacks as CoreMIDI ports
- USB audio both ways, through Mac audio devices
- Arturia MiniLab 3 support
- Clean power-off on quit, so the active project is saved
